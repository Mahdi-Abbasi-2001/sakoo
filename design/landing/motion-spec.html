Motion spec

The blueprint builds itself

What moves, what it says, when it starts, how long, how it eases, and what a person with reduced motion gets instead. Demos in the table are live.

  1. Every animation communicates state. If removing it loses no information, it is not in the spec.
  2. Apply and Decline never move or resize. The approval ring is an outline on a separate layer. Measured: one rectangle across a full 30 s loop.
  3. No layout shift. Numbers are tabular and fixed width. The approval box has a fixed height in every state. Only transform, opacity and stroke-dashoffset animate.
  4. Log text is never animated. Lines append instantly.
  5. CSS, SVG and the Web Animations API only. No library. The sheet is one script of about 300 lines.
  6. Reduced motion gets a still that tells the whole story: the Live frame plus five frame buttons, and a Pause button when motion is on.
  7. The tab hidden or the sheet off screen pauses everything: the clock stops and CSS animations are paused.
  8. Contrast stays at 4.5 to 1 or more in every frame. Text never fades; it appears at full opacity. Measured every 0.2 s over the loop.
  9. RTL is mirrored, not flipped: the scene geometry, the sweep origin, the flow direction and the chevrons mirror. Code, paths, URLs and identifiers stay left to right.
  10. Real data only. The replay is built from docs/samples. A step shorter than 100 ms in a sample gets a visible floor, listed below.

Orb: six states

Reading

Particles are drawn in toward the core (8 particles, 1.7 s, staggered), ring turns slowly (18 s). Glyph: lines.

run_started, phase analyze

Thinking

Two arcs turn one way (2.4 s), ring turns the other (7 s), core wobbles (2.2 s). Glyph: three dots.

phase plan, tool_call

Waiting for you

Slow breathing, scale 1 to 1.09 in 3.2 s. Glyph: diamond. Violet.

confirm_request, question

Working

Fast arc (0.9 s) and ring (3 s). Glyph: chevrons, mirrored in RTL. Sky blue.

phase deploy, step_started

Done

One burst ring, once, 1 s. Then still. Glyph: check. Lime.

run_finished ok

Failed

Two short jolts, 3 px, 0.5 s each, then still. Ring breaks into dashes. Glyph: X. Red.

run_finished not ok, step_failed

Easing

  • ease-out cubic-bezier(.2,.7,.2,1). Things arriving: outlines, numbers, bursts, page content.
  • ease-in-out cubic-bezier(.4,0,.2,1). Things that breathe: waiting orb, wobble.
  • linear Things that run continuously: flow, rotation, shimmer, progress sweep, the replay clock.
  • none Anything that carries text or a decision: logs, buttons, status words.

Durations

  • 120 / 200 / 300 ms Hover, transitions, page in/out. Nothing structural is slower than 300 ms.
  • 0.5 to 2.4 s State animations that tell a step is happening (draw, tick, create).
  • 1 to 3.2 s loops Continuous state cues: flow 1 s, waiting breath 3.2 s, approval ring 1.5 s.
